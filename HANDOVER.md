# BrightSmile: notes for the clinic staff

## Logging in

Go to `/wp-admin` on the clinic website and log in with your own username and password. Never share your password.

## Changing a treatment

1. In the left menu click **Services**.
2. Click the treatment name to open it.
3. Change the title, the text or the price, then click **Update**.
4. To add a new treatment, click **Add New**, fill it in and click **Publish**.

## Changing a doctor

1. In the left menu click **Doctors**.
2. Open the doctor, change the name, speciality or experience, then click **Update**.
3. To add a doctor, click **Add New** and **Publish**.

## Where to see appointments

Click **Appointments** in the left menu. The table shows the name, phone number, treatment and date of the latest 20 requests. Only administrators and receptionists can see this page.

Patients book on the **Book Appointment** page of the website. After they send the form they see a thank-you message, and the request appears in the Appointments page.

## Who can do what

- **Administrator:** everything.
- **Receptionist:** can see the Appointments page.
- Other logged-in users cannot see appointments.

## If something looks wrong

1. Reload the page once.
2. If the problem stays, write down the page address and what you did, and send it to your developer.
